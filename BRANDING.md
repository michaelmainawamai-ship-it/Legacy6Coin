# Legacy6Coin Official Branding

This document records the official visual identity of Legacy6Coin (L6C) and provides guidance for its consistent use and preservation. It is intended to support continuity, provenance, and accurate attribution across project materials and archives.

## Official Identity

- **Official Name:** Legacy6Coin
- **Symbol:** L6C
- **Official Sigil:** Legacy6Coin Flame Sigil
- **Founder:** Michael Wamai Maina Gatane

The official sigil is the flame emblem containing an integrated number 6, presented with the L6C wordmark and the text “LEGACY 6 COIN” in the supplied reference artwork. The sigil and wordmark together identify the project; they do not represent a claim about token performance, utility, or value.

## Symbolism

The following descriptions record the intended visual interpretation of the official identity:

- **The flame** represents resilience, renewal, and continuity through change. Its enduring form expresses the project’s commitment to preserving its identity and history.
- **The number 6** is integrated into the flame to make the numeral a defining part of the sigil rather than a separate decoration. It connects the visual mark to the “6” in the project name.
- **The L6C wordmark** is the concise written identifier for Legacy6Coin. It provides a legible project reference alongside the emblem, including where the full name is not repeated.

## Brand Philosophy

Legacy6Coin’s identity is intended to reflect four principles:

- **Resilience:** retain purpose and continuity through difficulty and change.
- **Transparency:** make project information, asset provenance, and revisions discoverable and clearly documented.
- **Stewardship:** preserve project materials responsibly and avoid misleading representations.
- **Legacy:** maintain an attributable record that can be understood and carried forward by future contributors.

These principles are statements of project intent, not guarantees of outcomes.

When distributing these files, retain their filenames and record any source, export, or revision information available. Do not treat a derivative export as the original source unless its provenance has been documented.

## Usage Guidelines

- Do not distort, skew, stretch, or otherwise deform the artwork.
- Do not alter the proportions or relative placement of the sigil, wordmark, and accompanying text.
- Do not recolor the artwork without documenting and approving the variation, including its purpose and provenance.
- Preserve attribution to **Legacy6Coin** whenever the assets are reproduced or redistributed.
- Prefer the original or highest-quality preserved asset; avoid repeated lossy conversion of raster files.
- Keep the asset filename and relevant version or source details with copies placed in other repositories or archives.
