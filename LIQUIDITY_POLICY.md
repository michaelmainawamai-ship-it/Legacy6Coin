# Legacy6Coin (L6C) Liquidity Policy

## Purpose

This policy establishes transparency standards for liquidity, treasury activity, founder-controlled wallets, and future token issuance.

Legacy6Coin prioritizes stewardship, accountability, and public documentation. This policy is intended to make material actions understandable and auditable by participants and reviewers.

## Current Status

- **Network Status:** Sepolia Verified
- **Mainnet Status:** Not Deployed
- **Archive Version:** Legacy6Coin Archive v1.0
- **Founder Wallet Balance Snapshot:** 659,668 L6C

The balance is a snapshot and may change due to transfers, burns, liquidity provisioning, or documented treasury actions. It should not be treated as a live balance or as a guarantee of future holdings.

## Liquidity Principles

Legacy6Coin's liquidity practices are guided by:

1. **Transparency** — material liquidity actions should be disclosed clearly.
2. **Accountability** — decisions and relevant transaction references should be traceable.
3. **Documentation** — records should be retained in public project documentation.
4. **Stewardship** — assets should be handled responsibly and consistently with disclosed purposes.
5. **Public Disclosure** — material changes should be communicated in accessible public channels.

Liquidity should never be managed in a way that intentionally misleads participants.

## Initial Liquidity Framework

No initial mainnet liquidity allocation has been finalized.

Future liquidity actions should include public disclosure of:

- Amount of L6C provided
- Amount of paired assets provided
- Destination pool
- Transaction references
- Purpose

## Founder Wallet Disclosure Rule

Founder-controlled transfers of 10,000 L6C or more should be publicly disclosed. Disclosures should identify the transfer amount, transaction reference when available, and purpose, while avoiding unsupported claims about the transaction.

Disclosure may occur through:

- GitHub Releases
- `DISCLOSURE_LEDGER.md`
- Governance updates
- Repository documentation

## Disclosure Ledger

Legacy6Coin maintains a public Disclosure Ledger to document material events, including:

- Liquidity additions
- Liquidity removals
- Liquidity migrations
- Treasury transfers
- Major founder wallet movements
- Ownership transfers
- Significant token issuance events

The ledger is a documentation record; entries do not by themselves establish a transaction's legal or technical effect. Relevant transaction hashes and addresses should be included when available.

## Treasury Transparency

Material treasury actions should be publicly documented whenever practical. Examples include:

- Liquidity provisioning
- Treasury transfers
- Ecosystem expenditures
- Reserve allocations

## Market Integrity

Legacy6Coin does not support:

- Wash trading
- Artificial volume generation
- Deceptive market activity
- Hidden treasury movements
- Misleading liquidity disclosures

## Risks

Liquidity does not guarantee:

- Market demand
- Price stability
- Utility
- Exchange listings

Digital assets involve risk. Participants should make their own assessments and should not rely on liquidity as an assurance of market performance.

## Future Revisions

This policy may evolve as the project develops. Material revisions should be documented publicly so readers can identify and review changes.
