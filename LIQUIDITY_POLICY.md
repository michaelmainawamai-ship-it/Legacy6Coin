# Legacy6Coin (L6C) Liquidity Policy

This policy sets out the project's public commitments for liquidity-related activity and treasury disclosures. It does not promise that liquidity will be created, maintained, or available.

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to make liquidity decisions understandable and reviewable by holders, auditors, exchanges, developers, and community members. Liquidity arrangements, if any, must be described accurately and must not be presented as assurance of price stability, demand, or exit availability.

## Guiding Principles

Legacy6Coin commits to:

- transparency about material liquidity and treasury actions;
- responsible stewardship and accurate, timely records;
- honest communication about uncertainty and risk; and
- market integrity rather than artificial activity or short-term hype.

## Supply Transparency

The stated maximum supply is 1,000,000 L6C, of which 666,000 L6C was initially minted and 334,000 L6C remains issuable under owner control within the cap. See [TOKENOMICS.md](TOKENOMICS.md) for allocation details. Liquidity positions do not change the supply cap. Transfers into a liquidity pool are not new issuance; any minting must be separately disclosed and remain within the contract's rules.

## Liquidity Objectives

If liquidity is considered, objectives may include enabling token swaps or supporting access to an ecosystem. These are possible purposes, not promises of future pools, exchange support, market depth, or token utility. Liquidity cannot guarantee fair pricing, stable markets, or the ability to sell at a particular time or price.

## Initial Liquidity Framework

Before a project-controlled initial liquidity contribution, the project will disclose, where applicable:

- the network and pool or protocol;
- the pool address and paired asset;
- the amounts and source of contributed assets;
- whether liquidity positions are locked, controlled, or transferable, and the relevant terms;
- the purpose and material risks; and
- transaction hashes and links to public on-chain records.

No liquidity event should be described as completed until its on-chain transaction can be independently reviewed. If no liquidity has been established, communications should say so plainly.

## Treasury Disclosure Rules

The project will not use undisclosed treasury wallets. Project-controlled treasury addresses should be identified publicly before or when they are used for material project activity. If an address's control or purpose changes, that change should be disclosed.

Major treasury transactions should include the date, asset, amount, sending and receiving addresses when appropriate, purpose, and transaction hash. Disclosures should distinguish verified on-chain facts from explanations or estimates. Sensitive information may be withheld only where disclosure would create a concrete security or legal risk; any such limitation should be explained, and the information should be disclosed when that risk no longer applies where feasible.

### Founder Wallet Disclosure Rule

Any movement exceeding 10,000 L6C from founder-controlled wallets should be disclosed publicly through repository updates, governance notices, or official communication channels.

The disclosure should identify the amount, transaction hash, destination where appropriate, and purpose or context. This rule is a project transparency commitment and does not change the on-chain permissions or impose a restriction enforced by the contract.

## Market Integrity Rules

The project and its representatives will not engage in or promote:

- wash trading;
- artificial volume generation;
- deceptive trading practices;
- undisclosed market manipulation; or
- misleading claims about liquidity, trading activity, or market support.

Public statements must not imply that liquidity guarantees a price, protects holders from loss, or assures a market.

## Public Disclosure Commitments

The project will publicly disclose:

1. liquidity additions, including project-controlled contributions;
2. liquidity removals, including withdrawals or material reductions in project-controlled positions; and
3. liquidity migrations, including movement to another pool, protocol, or network.

Disclosures will include transaction hashes or other public references when available, the amounts and assets involved, and a concise explanation. Material changes should be reported promptly through repository updates or official project communication channels.

## Risks

Liquidity can be limited, withdrawn, concentrated, or exposed to technical failures, smart-contract vulnerabilities, market volatility, and third-party actions. Pool participation may result in loss, including impermanent loss. Disclosures do not remove these risks or guarantee that a holder can exchange tokens.

## Future Amendments

Material changes to this policy will be recorded in the repository with an updated review date and communicated through official channels where available. Amendments do not retroactively alter on-chain transactions or create guarantees about future liquidity. The current policy version in this repository is authoritative for the project's stated commitments.
