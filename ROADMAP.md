# Legacy6Coin (L6C) Roadmap

This roadmap records areas of planned work, not guarantees of completion, deployment, listings, or outcomes. Priorities and timing may change as security, technical, regulatory, and operational reviews progress.

## Phase 1 – Foundation

- Complete and maintain public source verification for the Sepolia deployment.
- Establish clear project documentation and public records.
- Conduct appropriate security reviews and address findings before considering mainnet deployment.
- Form a community with transparent, responsible communication practices.

## Phase 2 – Mainnet Readiness

- Harden administrative and operational wallets, with documented access and recovery practices.
- Plan any liquidity activity and its public disclosure in accordance with [LIQUIDITY_POLICY.md](LIQUIDITY_POLICY.md).
- Develop governance plans that accurately describe existing owner permissions and any proposed changes.
- Prepare ecosystem documentation, deployment procedures, monitoring, and incident-response processes.

Readiness work does not itself authorize deployment. A mainnet decision should follow technical, security, operational, and legal review appropriate to the project.

## Phase 3 – Mainnet Review

**Target review milestone: January 14, 2027 (God willing).**

This is a target date to review project readiness and outstanding work. It is **not** a guaranteed deployment date, launch announcement, or commitment to deploy. Deployment may occur later, or may not occur, depending on review results and other considerations. Any mainnet deployment status will be stated separately and supported with verifiable contract and transaction references.

## Phase 4 – Ecosystem Development

Subject to readiness and available resources, areas for exploration include:

- exploring practical utility without implying that utility is guaranteed;
- producing educational content about the contract, risks, and project decisions;
- supporting measured, transparent community growth; and
- publishing transparency reports about material supply, treasury, liquidity, and governance actions.

These are areas of interest rather than promised deliverables. Updates should clearly distinguish completed work from proposals and future possibilities.
