# Legacy6Coin (L6C) Tokenomics

## Supply facts

| Supply item | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Maximum Supply | 1,000,000 L6C |
| Founder Allocation | 660,000 L6C |
| Witness Allocations | 6,000 L6C |
| Initial Minted Supply | 666,000 L6C |
| Remaining Issuable Supply | 334,000 L6C |

The initial minted supply is the sum of the founder allocation and witness allocations. Remaining issuable supply is the difference between the maximum supply and initial minted supply; it is not an allocation or a commitment to issue tokens.

## Issuance disclosure

As of Legacy6Coin Archive v1.0, the remaining 334,000 L6C has not been formally allocated. No commitment currently exists regarding its future use. Any future issuance should be publicly disclosed.

## Status and scope

These figures describe the current documented Legacy6CoinV2 supply facts. Mainnet is not deployed. This document does not imply that remaining issuable supply will be issued, nor does it make claims about future value or market outcomes. Material issuance events should be recorded in the [Disclosure Ledger](DISCLOSURE_LEDGER.md).

See [TREASURY_POLICY.md](TREASURY_POLICY.md) for the policy governing the unallocated reserve and future issuance disclosures.
