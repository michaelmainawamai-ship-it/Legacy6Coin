# Legacy6Coin (L6C) Treasury Policy

**Project:** Legacy6Coin (L6C)  
**Founder:** Michael Wamai Maina Gatane  
**Archive reference:** Legacy6Coin Archive v1.0

## Purpose

This document establishes principles for future issuance decisions, treasury transparency, and stewardship of unallocated token capacity. The treasury policy exists to promote transparency and reduce uncertainty by making the current position, decision authority, and disclosure expectations clear.

This policy documents governance commitments and recommended practices. It does not itself execute or authorize an on-chain transaction, alter contract permissions, or commit tokens to a future use.

## Treasury Principles

1. **Transparency** — Material treasury and issuance decisions should be communicated clearly, with relevant supporting information.
2. **Accountability** — Decisions and transactions should be traceable to a documented rationale, authorized action, and available on-chain evidence.
3. **Stewardship** — Any assets or issuance capacity under project control should be handled responsibly and consistently with disclosed purposes.
4. **Documentation** — Material proposals, decisions, transactions, and changes should be recorded in the appropriate project documents and archives.
5. **Public Disclosure** — Future issuance and material treasury actions should be disclosed publicly before or shortly after execution, as circumstances permit.
6. **Long-Term Sustainability** — Decisions should account for operational continuity, project obligations, and the long-term preservation of records, without implying guaranteed outcomes.

## Treasury Allocation Process

Before any future issuance, the following steps are recommended:

1. Document the proposed allocation, including amount, recipient, and intended timing.
2. Record the rationale and any relevant considerations, limitations, or dependencies.
3. Publish a disclosure describing the proposal before execution where practicable; if advance disclosure is not practicable, explain why and publish promptly after execution.
4. Execute the transaction only through the contract's authorized process and within its supply limit.
5. Record the transaction hash and verify the resulting on-chain event and supply information.
6. Update `DISCLOSURE_LEDGER.md` and any other relevant project documents.

A proposal is not an approval, and a disclosure entry does not by itself establish the transaction's legal or technical effect. Transaction evidence should be included when available.

## Treasury Disclosure Rule

Any issuance from the remaining 334,000 L6C reserve should include:

- Amount
- Recipient
- Purpose
- Transaction hash
- Date
- Supporting reference

If a field is not yet available at the time of an initial disclosure, identify it as pending and update the record when confirmed. Do not publish private keys, seed phrases, or other sensitive access information.

## Prohibited Treasury Practices

Legacy6Coin does not support:

- Hidden allocations
- Undisclosed issuance
- Misleading treasury disclosures
- Fabricated allocation explanations
- Secret treasury wallets intended to conceal material actions

Treasury records and disclosures should not be used to imply approvals, allocations, or completed actions that have not occurred.
